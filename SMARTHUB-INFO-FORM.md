## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### For Teachers

**Q: What are the minimum booking requirements?**
A: No minimum for occasional use. Regular partners typically book 4+ hours per week for better rates.

**Q: Can I bring my existing students?**
A: Absolutely! Most teachers bring their established student base. We also help you grow through referrals.

**Q: What if I need to cancel a booking?**
A: With 3+ days notice, 80% refund. See full cancellation policy above. Monthly partners have more flexibility.

**Q: Do you provide marketing support?**
A: Yes! Website listing, social media promotion, and student referrals for partner teachers.

**Q: Can I reserve the same time slot weekly?**
A: Yes, monthly contract holders can reserve recurring time slots with priority access.

---

### For Parents/Students

**Q: How do I know which teacher is right for my child?**
A: Our initial consultation includes teacher matching based on your child's needs, learning style, and goals.

**Q: What if my child doesn't improve?**
A: We monitor progress closely and adjust teaching approach as needed. Consultation included in all programs.

**Q: Are teachers qualified and vetted?**
A: Yes, all teachers are carefully screened for qualifications, experience, and teaching effectiveness.

**Q: Can I schedule a trial lesson before committing?**
A: Yes, most teachers offer trial sessions. Ask during your consultation about trial options.

**Q: What's included in the monthly program price?**
A: Teaching sessions (16 hours/month), materials, progress monitoring, and educational consultation support.

**Q: Do you offer online lessons?**
A: Yes, many of our teachers offer hybrid or fully online options. Discuss preferences during consultation.

---

### For Corporate Clients

**Q: Can training be delivered at our office?**
A: Yes, we offer on-site delivery at your location or at our facility - your choice.

**Q: How do you customize training to our business needs?**
A: We conduct needs assessment, consult with your team, and design programs addressing your specific goals.

**Q: What's the typical group size for corporate training?**
A: 6-12 participants per session for optimal interaction and results. Larger groups can be accommodated.

**Q: Do you provide training materials?**
A: Yes, all materials and resources included in training programs.

**Q: Can we get post-training support?**
A: Yes, follow-up coaching and support can be included in training packages.
