# Grade 7 English - Curriculum Outline

**Source:** Let's Learn English - 7th Year Basic Education Teacher's Book
**Published by:** Republic of Tunisia, Ministry of Education, National Pedagogic Centre
**Date Analyzed:** November 6, 2025

---

## Overview

### Textbook Structure
- **Format:** Modular structure with 5 modules
- **Each Module Contains:**
  - 4 input sections
  - 1 review section
- **Components:**
  - Student's Book
  - Activity Book
  - Teacher's Book
  - Course tape (audio)

### Pedagogical Approach
- **Method:** Principled eclecticism (combining multiple teaching approaches)
- **Philosophy:** Learner-centered, communicative approach
- **Key Principles:**
  - Language as communication tool
  - Integration of all skills (reading, writing, listening, speaking)
  - Meaningful contexts for language learning
  - Discovery learning and problem-solving
  - Minimal stress, maximum engagement

### Module Progression (Expanding Outward)
The book progresses from self to outer world:
1. **Self** → 2. **Immediate Environment** → 3. **Outside World (Others)**

**Story Framework:** A British teenager (Peter) stays with a Tunisian family (Aly's family) for cultural exchange, facilitating intercultural learning.

## Grammar Progression

### Verb Tenses
1. **Simple Present**
   - To be (affirmative, negative, interrogative)
   - Have/Has got
   - Action verbs (like, love, enjoy, etc.)
   - Third person -s
   - Adverbs of frequency

2. **Present Progressive**
   - Affirmative, negative, interrogative forms
   - Contrast with simple present

3. **Simple Past** (Introduction)
   - Regular verbs (-ed endings)
   - Irregular verbs (common ones)
   - Was/Were

### Modal Verbs
- Can/Can't (ability, permission, requests)
- Must/Mustn't (obligation, prohibition)
- Would like (polite requests)

### Question Formation
- Yes/No questions
- WH-questions: What, Where, When, How many, How much, How old
- Question words with different tenses

### Other Structures
- Demonstratives (this/these, that/those)
- Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, etc.)
- Object pronouns (me, you, him, her, us, them)
- Articles (a, an, the)
- Prepositions of time (at, in, on)
- Prepositions of place (in, on, under, behind, between, next to, etc.)
- There is/There are
- Genitive 's (possession)
- Imperatives
- Linkers (and, but, because, then)

### Word Formation
- Adjective + ly = Adverb (slow/slowly, quick/quickly)
- Noun + y = Adjective (sun/sunny, rain/rainy)
- Verb + er = Noun (teach/teacher, speak/speaker)
- Regular and irregular plurals

## Teaching Methodology Notes

### Lesson Structure (Typical)
1. **Warm-up/Engagement** - Activate prior knowledge
2. **Presentation** - Introduce new language in context
3. **Controlled Practice** - Guided activities with support
4. **Consolidation** - Activity Book exercises
5. **Production** - Student-generated language use
6. **Game/Fun Activity** - Motivating closure

### Recommended Approaches
- **Learner-centered:** Students actively construct learning
- **Communicative:** Focus on meaning and interaction
- **Task-based:** Real-world tasks and purposes
- **Eclectic:** Drawing from multiple methodologies
- **Skills integration:** All four skills in every session

### Key Principles
- Meaningful contexts for language
- Interaction and communication
- Problem-solving and discovery
- Creativity and autonomy
- Minimal stress, maximum engagement
- Variety in activities and techniques
- Recycling and spiraling of content

### Time Allocation
- Each module: approximately 10 teaching hours
- Remaining time for: consolidation, remedial work, project work
- Flexible pacing based on learner needs

## Conclusion

The Grade 7 English curriculum builds on Grade 6 foundations while introducing more complex structures and expanding vocabulary.
