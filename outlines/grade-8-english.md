# Grade 8 English Curriculum Outline

**Source:** Let's Discover More English - 8th Year Basic Education Teacher's Book
**Publisher:** Republic of Tunisia, Ministry of Education, National Pedagogic Centre
**Document:** 8_prim-en.pdf (54 pages)

---

## Overview

### Textbook Structure
- **Format:** 5 modules with 5 lessons each
- **Total Lessons:** 25 lessons across the academic year
- **Approach:** Story-driven with communicative language teaching methodology
- **Components:** Student's Book, Activity Book, Teacher's Book, Audio CD/Cassette

### Pedagogical Approach
- **Learning as discovery:** Students discover more about English and themselves
- **Learner-centered:** Students at the core of the teaching-learning process
- **Skills integration:** Reading, writing, listening, and speaking practiced together
- **Project-based learning:** Incorporated as major component
- **Process-oriented:** Focus on both product and learning processes
- **Self-evaluation:** Built-in assessment at end of each module

### Lesson Structure
Each lesson follows a three-part structure:
1. **Let's Get Ready:** Activate prior knowledge and set objectives
2. **Let's Discover:** Engage in discovering new content
3. **Let's Communicate:** Reinvest learning to produce oral/written texts

## Grammar Progression Summary

### Tenses
1. **Review:** Present simple, present progressive, past simple
2. **NEW:** Future with "will" (Module 1)
3. **NEW:** "be going to" for future plans (Module 2)
4. **NEW:** Past progressive (Module 3)

### Modal Verbs and Expressions
1. **Review:** "can" for ability
2. **NEW:** "will be able to" for future ability (Module 1)
3. **NEW:** "should" for advice (Module 2)
4. **NEW:** "could" for past ability (Module 5)

### Question Forms
1. **Review:** Yes/no questions, wh-questions
2. **NEW:** "How long...?" (Module 2)
3. **NEW:** "How far/how often/etc." (Module 4)
4. **NEW:** "Whose...?" (Module 5)

### Pronouns
1. **Review:** Personal pronouns, demonstratives
2. **NEW:** Relative pronouns (who, which, where, when) - Module 4
3. **NEW:** Reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself, etc.) - Module 5
4. **NEW:** Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, etc.) - Module 5

### Other Structures
1. **NEW:** Gerund (-ing as noun) - Module 3
2. **NEW:** "spend + time + -ing" - Module 3
3. **NEW:** Comparative for gradual change - Module 3
4. **NEW:** Compound nouns (N+N, V-ing+N) - Modules 2, 4, 5
5. **NEW:** Compound adjectives (numeral-noun) - Module 4
6. **NEW:** Sequential adverbs (first, then, next, finally) - Module 1
7. **NEW:** Expressions of addition and opposition - Module 5
