## CURRICULUM OVERVIEW

### Course Structure
- **6 Thematic Modules**
- **5 Lessons per Module** (30 lessons total)
- **Skills Focus:** Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking
- **Materials Package:** Student's Book, Activity Book, Audio Cassette/CD, Teacher's Book

### Pedagogical Principles
1. **Learner-Centered Approach:** Student is an active user of English at the core of the learning process
2. **Constructiveness:** Teaching based on learner's active construction and higher-level knowledge structures
3. **Cooperativeness:** Collaborative learning in groups, sharing knowledge
4. **Contextuality:** Language as communication, not decontextualized grammar

### Lesson Structure
#### Student's Book (3 Parts):
1. **Get Ready:** Topic introduction through pictures, brainstorming, background knowledge
2. **Learn More About It:** Reading/listening passages, poems, conversations with comprehension questions
3. **The Grammar Point:** Grammar structures from reading/listening material, contextualized learning

#### Activity Book (4-5 Parts):
1. **Comprehension Check:** Further comprehension and written expression
2. **Language Work:** Grammar and vocabulary practice through writing
3. **Pronunciation:** Sounds, spelling, stress exercises
4. **Writing and/or Project Work:** Multi-step tasks requiring cooperation
5. **The Lighter Side/Grandma's Corner:** Relaxation activities

## MODULE 1: FAMILY LIFE

### Module Overview
**Theme:** Family relationships, responsibilities, generation gap, pocket money, safety at home
**Project:** Multi-step project about school problems (fashion through generations)
**Duration:** Approximately 2-3 weeks

### LESSON 1: Family Relationships
**Main Skills:** Reading, Writing, Speaking
**Objective:** Enable students to talk about family relationships and realize family life is not always happy

**Subskills:**
- Read for gist
- Scan for specific information

**Activities:**
- Discuss family patterns (now and before)
- Read three texts about different family relationships
- Compare different family situations
- Project Work: Step 1

## METHODOLOGICAL NOTES FOR TEACHERS

### Guiding Principles
1. **Learner is intelligent:** Activate appropriate learning strategies
2. **Learner-centered:** Active construction of knowledge
3. **Participation and interaction:** Cooperative learning in groups
4. **Contextuality:** Language as communication in authentic/simulated situations

### Teaching Approach
- Grammar stems from reading/listening material (contextualized)
- Visuals help trigger imagination and activate background knowledge
- Skills integrated within lessons
- Balance between fluency and accuracy
- Regular assessment through project work and writing tasks

### Flexibility
- Timeline suggestions may require adjustments
- Teachers develop own annual schedule
- Teacher's book provides hints, not rigid instructions
- "This book is merely intended to help teachers and should in no way enslave them"
